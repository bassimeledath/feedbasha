Personality, type, and color

Three coherent directions to align on before production styling. Direction A is my recommendation.

A · Recommended
Sharp, helpful, and a little amused.

Constructive Karen

Direct microcopy, never mean. Instrument Sans feels human and precise without looking like generic AI software.

B · Calm
Clear feedback, no theatrics.

Calm critic

Spline Sans is warmer and rounder. The petrol-and-coral palette feels capable with a small spark of humor.

C · Expressive
I have a few notes.

Editorial wit

A display serif used only for rare headings, paired with a sans for controls. Memorable, but easiest to overdo.

Decision needed

Pick one icon number and one personality direction. They can be mixed—for example, icon 02 with direction A. Production work should then turn the chosen image concept into a deterministic SVG, define tokens, and tune copy around that single voice.
